Role Overview
What Does an Intake Coordinator Do?
Central Coordination Hub
Serves as the primary point of coordination between referral sources, clients, caregivers, clinicians, physicians, and internal teams — ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.
End-to-End Referral Management
Manages every referral from receipt through admission — including intake, eligibility verification, clinician assignment, documentation transfer, and client communication.
Regulatory & Payer Compliance
Ensures all processes adhere to regulatory requirements, payer-specific admission standards, and agency policies — protecting the organization from compliance risk.
Proactive Escalation
Identifies and escalates barriers immediately — clinician unavailability, authorization delays, insurance issues, or any threat to timely and safe admission of the client.
